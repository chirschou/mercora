## Creating an Account

Click “Sign In / Register” in the header to sign in or create an account. Accounts are provided through our authentication provider, Clerk. You can also check out as a guest without an account.

## Password Help

Forgot your password? Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. Clerk handles passwords; Melt Party never sees them.

## Your Account

Once signed in, your account shows your orders (https://melt.party/account/orders), saved addresses, saved payment methods, subscriptions and profile settings. Account deletion and data export are not self-service: email support@melt.party from the address linked to your account with the subject line “Privacy request”.

## Email Issues

Check your spam folder if you don’t receive login or order confirmation emails.
