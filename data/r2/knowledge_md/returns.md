**AI NOTES:** Use this to explain return windows, conditions, and refund processing. Do not accept returns outside this policy. There is no self-service return portal: returns start by email.

## Return Policy

We accept returns of unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery.

## Return Instructions

- Email support@melt.party with your order number to start a return.
- We reply with return instructions and, for eligible returns within the United States, a prepaid return label.
- Pack items securely.
- Do not send returns to our company address; it does not accept returns.

If you have an account, your orders and their status are at https://melt.party/account/orders. If you checked out as a guest, use the order status link in your confirmation email; those links expire, so if yours has, email us with your order number.

## Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

Contact us as soon as possible after delivery. We cover the return shipping and, at your choice, replace the item or refund it.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we issue the refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Any amount you paid with a gift card is returned to that gift card. Your bank may take additional time to show the credit.

## Exclusions

Clearance and final-sale items, gift cards, and opened food or other consumable items are not eligible for return.

The full policy is in Section 8 of our Terms of Service: https://melt.party/terms-of-service.
